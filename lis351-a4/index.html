02 / Put it into practice

Start small. Make it repeatable.

These fictional examples show how to turn a general idea into a simple plan.

An example month

Give each dollar a purpose.

Imagine $1,200 available after taxes. Here is one possible breakdown for practice:

Essential expenses
$800
Optional spending
$250
Savings
$150
Total
$1,200

Your own amounts will depend on your income and expenses.

A goal with a timeline

Turn “someday” into a plan.

For a fictional $300 goal over six months, setting aside $50 each month reaches the target, ignoring interest.

$50per month × 6 months = $300

Give the goal a name, track the amount saved, and revisit the timeline if your situation changes.

03 / Keep the habit

Your weekly money check.

Set aside a few minutes to keep your plan up to date.

  1. Review the purchases you made this week.
  2. Check which bills are coming up next.
  3. Record your progress toward your savings goal.
  4. Choose one adjustment for the coming week.
